General Relativity (GR) describes gravity as smooth spacetime curvature, while Quantum Mechanics (QM) operates on discrete probabilistic state updates at Planck intervals. **Dark Matter:** Galactic rotation curves, gravitational lensing, and CMB anisotropies require Ωm ≈ 0.27-0.31 [6,10], yet no dark matter particle has been detected despite extensive searches [11]. The required density parameter remains unexplained by Standard Model physics.

**Dark Energy:** Supernova observations reveal accelerating expansion [12] requiring Ωv ≈ 0.68-0.70 [6,13]. The nature of this "vacuum energy" driving acceleration remains unknown. Recent DESI observations suggest dark energy may evolve over time [9,14], challenging the cosmological constant interpretation.
